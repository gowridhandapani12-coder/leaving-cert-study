Top exam tips for this chapter

  1. For every indicator, say what it measures, then how a change affects a named business.
  2. Use a chain: indicator changes → cost or demand changes → effect on profit, jobs or growth.
  3. Say which direction: rising rates hurt borrowers but help savers. One change can help one business and hurt another.
  4. Exchange rates: a stronger euro = dearer exports, cheaper imports. Learn this both ways.
  5. Growth is about output (GDP); development is about quality of life. Don't mix them up.
  6. For Ireland, mention GNI*: GDP is inflated by multinational profits.
  7. The business economy: use turnover and employment, and compare SMEs with large and foreign-owned firms.
  8. Show workings in calculations and put €, % or £/$ on every answer.
