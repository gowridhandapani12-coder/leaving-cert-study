Learning outcomes

Students learn about:

  • the different types of business that operate, including both public, private, semi-state and not for profit enterprises.
  • the nature of business ownership and how this may evolve over the lifecycle of a business (including nationalisation and privatisation). The types of organisations explored should include sole traders, partnerships, private limited companies, public listed companies, franchises, co-operatives and public sector organisations.
  • how organisations are regulated both internally and externally including audit, risk management and compliance.
  • the significance of Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) factors in business governance.

Top exam tips for this chapter

  1. Use State, Explain, Example: name the point, explain it in a full sentence, then give an example.
  2. When comparing forms of business, compare on the same feature: owners, liability, finance, control, profit.
  3. Learn limited vs unlimited liability word-perfect. It's the most-asked difference.
  4. For public vs private vs not for profit, always say who owns it and what it aims for.
  5. Nationalisation and privatisation: give the direction (State → private or private → State) and an Irish example.
  6. Regulation: split it into internal (audit, risk management, compliance) and external (named regulators).
  7. ESG: name all three factors and give an example action for each.
  8. Show workings in calculations and put €, % or c on every answer.
