Top exam tips for this chapter

  1. Use State, Explain, Example: name the point, explain it in a full sentence, then give an example.
  2. Always name the stakeholder. "The bank, as an investor, wants…" beats "they want…".
  3. Give both sides: what the stakeholder gives the business and what it wants back.
  4. For conflict, say who clashes, why (their different goals) and how to resolve it.
  5. When a question has a case study, quote or refer to it in every point.
  6. "Evaluate" or "discuss" needs a judgement: finish with your opinion and a reason.
  7. Stakeholder maps: label both axes (power, interest) and all four quadrants.
  8. Show workings in calculations and put € or % on every answer.
