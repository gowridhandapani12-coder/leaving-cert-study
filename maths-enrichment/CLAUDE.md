# Maths Enrichment (beyond the Leaving Cert)

Interactive pages for strong students who want maths beyond the Leaving Certificate: olympiad techniques and
the Irish Mathematical Olympiad. Same design and page format as the other subjects (see `../physics/CLAUDE.md`
for the format rules and quality checks, and `../CLAUDE.md` for the site structure).

Course progress and build order: `COURSE-PLAN.md`. Update it whenever a page is built.

Built so far: `barycentric-coordinates.html` (1 Barycentric coordinates), `olympiad.html` (2 The Maths Olympiad).
The hub `index.html` is generated by `node tools/site.js` (subject "maths-enrichment" in `SUBJECTS`).

## Run it locally

`node server.js` in this folder → http://localhost:5185 (preview config "mathsenrichment"), or the whole site with
`node server.js` at the project root (port 5190).

## Sources

- There is no official IrMO syllabus. irmo.ie says problems "broadly fall into" algebra, number theory, discrete
  mathematics, inequalities and geometry, and the enrichment classes have "little overlap" with the Leaving Cert.
  Competition facts (Round 1 dates, the six senior centres, IrMO final 23 January 2027, squad of about twelve,
  IMO 2026 Shanghai 10–20 July, EGMO 2026 Bordeaux 9–15 April, team sizes) are from https://www.irmo.ie/
  (pages: index, Round-1, SME, imo, egmo, problems, resources), checked 20 November 2026. Re-check each year.
- Past problems: IrMO 2009 papers with solutions on irmo.ie (`/IrMO2009paper1solutions.pdf`, `paper2`), readable with
  the pdf.js-in-the-browser method; the collected IrMO problems 1988–2023 (Mark Dukes) at
  https://maths.ucd.ie/~dukes/Irish_Maths_Olympiad_Compendium.pdf; IMO problems at https://www.imo-official.org/problems.aspx.
  Quote problem statements exactly with year, paper and number; write hints and solutions yourself (never copy
  official solutions); verify every answer (brute force where possible) and label them "our solutions".
- Barycentric page: built from the user's PDF, Zachary Abel, *Barycentric Coordinates* (notes, 17 August 2007),
  explained in our own words and credited on the page. Do not reproduce the notes' text.

## Page format notes

- Pages were built by splicing shared code from `../applied-maths/s1-1-mathematical-modelling.html` between fixed
  markers, then adding page data and labs (see the Applied Maths notes).
- Trainers: barycentric = decimal or fraction answers with tight tolerance; olympiad = whole-number answers.
  The olympiad page has a "Past problems" lab (hint, answer check, full solution, solved progress saved as `meol-solved`).
- localStorage prefixes `mebc-` and `meol-`; theme/font keys `me-theme` / `me-font`.
