Unit I.3 · Basic communicative proficiency

¿Qué tiempo hace?
¿Qué tiempo hará?
¡Han dicho que va a nevar!

Weather comes up in every part of the exam: postcards and emails from holidays, forecasts on the listening paper, and "¿Cómo es el clima en Irlanda?" in the oral. Learn hace, hay and está, the stem-changing weather verbs, the map of Spain, how to read and pass on a forecast, and how to ask whether a plan will still work in that weather.

    Labs

    Describe the sky, change the weather verbs, read the map of Spain, collect forecast data, pass on a forecast and decide what you can still do.

    ¿Qué tiempo hace?

    Look at the sky. Choose the sentence that describes it correctly: hace, hay, está or a weather verb.

    ¿Qué tiempo hace?
    1Do all fourteen. Which weather words go with hace, which with hay, which with está?
    2Why is it "hace buen tiempo" and not "hace bueno tiempo"?
    3Describe today's weather where you are in two sentences.
    Llueve, nieva, truena

    Pick a weather verb and a tense. Watch the stem change light up in the present, and disappear in every other tense.

    Verbo
    Tiempo verbal
    1Compare llover and nevar in the present. Which vowel changes in each?
    2Switch to the future and the imperfect. Is there still a stem change?
    3Why are weather verbs only used in the él / ella form?
    El mapa del tiempo

    Read each line of the forecast. Tap the part of Spain it is about. Learn the regions and the points of the compass as you go.

    Pronóstico
    1Finish the whole forecast. Which regions are "el norte", "el sur", "el este" and "el noroeste"?
    2Find the cordillera that separates Spain from France.
    3Say where you live in Ireland using a compass point: Vivo en el oeste de Irlanda.
    Collect & fit

    Read tomorrow's forecast for towns at different heights. Pick the maximum temperature you hear in words, and plot it against altitude.

    ¿Cuál será la máxima?
    Datos
    1Collect eight towns. By how many degrees does it get colder for every 1 000 m you climb?
    2Use your line: what will the maximum be at 3 000 m (the top of Sierra Nevada is about 3 480 m)?
    3Say one forecast aloud: "En Ávila la máxima será de… grados."
    ¿Qué han dicho?

    Pass the forecast on to someone else. Complete each sentence in indirect speech: ha dicho que…, han anunciado que…

    Choose
    1Get all twelve. After "han dicho que", does the forecast's tense change?
    2Find the one sentence where the future turns into the conditional. What's different about it?
    3Tell a friend tomorrow's forecast: "Han dicho que mañana…"
    ¿Se podrá?

    Read the forecast and the plan. Will the activity be possible? Decide, then read the model answer with si + present, future.

    ¿Se podrá?
    1Do all twelve. Which weather words told you "no"?
    2Make your own rule: "Si hace viento, no podremos…"
    3Ask about an activity politely: "¿Sabe usted si se podrá esquiar mañana?"

    Get exam ready

    Exam prep

    How Spanish is assessed, what this unit means for the oral and the written paper, and practice with feedback.

    Speaking · 25%

    Oral: general conversation (you may discuss a literary work) and a role-play. Expect "¿Qué tiempo hace en Irlanda?" and questions on holidays.

    Listening · 20%

    Radio forecasts, traffic and travel news, phone messages about the weather; answer in English or Irish.

    Reading · 30%

    Forecasts, weather maps, news about storms, heatwaves and floods.

    Writing · 25%

    Postcards, emails and diary entries from holidays always mention the weather. (Ordinary Level: 20 / 25 / 40 / 15.)

    Top exam tips for this unit

    1. Learn the three patterns: hace sol / calor / frío / viento, hay niebla / tormenta, está nublado / despejado.
    2. The weather verbs change their stem in the present: llueve, nieva, truena, hiela. Spell them right every time.
    3. In a postcard, use three tenses: ayer llovió, hoy hace sol, mañana hará calor.
    4. Show range with mucho and un poco de: hace mucho calor, hay un poco de niebla. Never "muy calor".
    5. Listening: forecasts are fast. Listen for the region, the sky and the temperature, in that order.
    6. Pass on a forecast with Han dicho que + future: Han dicho que mañana lloverá.
    7. For the oral, prepare "¿Cómo es el clima en Irlanda?": templado, húmedo, llueve mucho, nunca hace mucho calor ni mucho frío.
    8. Check the SEC for the current exam arrangements. Questions here are practice questions, not official SEC papers.
