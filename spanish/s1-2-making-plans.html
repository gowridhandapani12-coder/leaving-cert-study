Unit I.2 · Basic communicative proficiency

¿Quedamos el sábado?
¿Qué vas a hacer?
¡Te lo prometo!

Plans are everywhere in the exam: the oral asks what you'll do after the Leaving Cert, the written paper asks you to invite, arrange and apologise. Learn the future tense and ir a, say what you hope and intend, offer and promise, make and change arrangements, and give your reasons.

    Get exam ready

    Exam prep

    How Spanish is assessed, what this unit means for the oral and the written paper, and practice with feedback.

    Speaking · 25%

    Oral: general conversation (you may discuss a literary work) and a role-play. Plans for the future come up in almost every conversation.

    Listening · 20%

    Messages and phone calls that make or change arrangements; answer in English or Irish.

    Reading · 30%

    Emails, chats and articles about plans, studies and careers.

    Writing · 25%

    Invitations, replies, notes changing plans, and opinions. (Ordinary Level: 20 / 25 / 40 / 15.)

    Top exam tips for this unit

    1. Prepare a strong answer to "¿Qué vas a hacer el año que viene?": course, place, reason, and a back-up plan.
    2. Use three future forms to show range: voy a…, pienso…, me gustaría…, plus one future tense verb (haré, tendré).
    3. Learn the irregular future stems: tendr-, har-, saldr-, podr-, vendr-, dir-, pondr-, sabr-, querr-, habr-.
    4. Give reasons: porque me interesa…, para + infinitive, para que + subjunctive.
    5. When changing plans in writing, apologise, explain and suggest: Lo siento, es que… ¿Lo dejamos para…?
    6. Offers to adults use usted: ¿Le ayudo? ¿Quiere que se lo traiga?
    7. One subjunctive used correctly (¿Quieres que lo haga yo? / Es importante que…) stands out at Higher Level.
    8. Check the SEC for the current exam arrangements. Questions here are practice questions, not official SEC papers.
