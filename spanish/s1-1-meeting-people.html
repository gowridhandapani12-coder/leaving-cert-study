    Exam prep

    How Spanish is assessed, what this unit means for the oral, and practice with feedback.

    Speaking · 25%

    Oral: general conversation (you may discuss a literary work) and a role-play. 15 minutes.

    Listening · 20%

    Recorded conversations, announcements and radio; answer in English or Irish.

    Reading · 30%

    Signs, letters, articles and literary texts.

    Writing · 25%

    Expressing feelings, giving information, describing, explaining. (Ordinary Level: 20 / 25 / 40 / 15.)

    Top exam tips for this unit

    1. The oral almost always starts here: have polished answers about you, your family, your home area and your school.
    2. Address the examiner as usted unless told otherwise: "¿Puede repetir, por favor?"
    3. Extend every answer: fact + detail + opinion. "Tengo dos hermanos. Mi hermano mayor estudia en Dublín. Nos llevamos muy bien."
    4. Learn numbers and dates perfectly: ages, phone numbers and times are tested in the aural.
    5. Make adjectives agree: irlandés/irlandesa, simpático/simpática.
    6. Every question opens with ¿ and every question word has an accent: ¿dónde?, ¿cómo?, ¿qué?
    7. In written tasks, use the right register: "Querido/a…" and tú for a friend; "Estimado/a señor/a…" and usted for a formal letter.
    8. Check the SEC for the current exam arrangements. Questions here are practice questions, not official SEC papers.
