## I.1 contents

Labs: ¿Tú o usted? (12 people on a singular/plural × informal/formal grid) · Question wheel (15 answers, 12 question
words) · Mi ficha (live ID card and paragraph: numbers, veintiún, nationality agreement, y/e) · El árbol familiar
(11-person tree quiz) · Collect & fit (read Spanish interview answers, record study vs work hours, fitted line) ·
¿Qué dices? (12 social situations). 16 performance targets and linguistic skills, 27 flashcards, 16 quiz questions,
53 glossary entries, typed grammar trainer (5 types), "who's being addressed?" drill, 3 practice tasks (email, oral
conversation, reading comprehension).

## I.2 contents

Labs: La máquina del futuro (15 verbs incl. 9 irregular stems × 6 persons; future, ir a + inf., conditional; stem + ending
blocks, sentence with English) · ¿Deseo o promesa? (12 sentences into wish / hope / plan / promise) · ¿Te ayudo? (12 situations:
offers, promises, reassurance, degrees of willingness, tú/usted) · Mi agenda (8 messages from Nerea: tap the diary slot to add,
move or cancel) · Collect & fit (invented students' study plans read in Spanish; total years of study against age starting
work, fitted line, slope about 1) · ¿Porque o para que? (12 gap-fills: por qué / porque / para que, hay que, es importante (que)
+ subjunctive). 7 performance targets + 5 linguistic skills, 27 flashcards, 16 quiz questions, 62 glossary entries, typed
grammar trainer (future tense, ir a, ¿quieres que…? + subjunctive, pronoun order), "What is the speaker doing?" drill (16),
3 practice tasks (email, oral, reading). localStorage prefix `es12-`.

## I.3 contents

Labs: ¿Qué tiempo hace? (15 weather pictures drawn on canvas; pick the right sentence with hace / hay / está or a weather verb) ·
Llueve, nieva, truena (6 weather verbs × 6 tenses, stem change highlighted in the present) · El mapa del tiempo (schematic map of
Spain with 9 regions incl. Baleares and a Canarias inset; tap the region for 10 forecast lines; weather icons fill the map) ·
Collect & fit (invented forecasts for 16 towns at real, rounded altitudes; read the maximum in words, plot against altitude,
slope ≈ −6.5 °C per 1 000 m) · ¿Qué han dicho? (12 indirect-speech gap-fills, options shuffled) · ¿Se podrá? (12 forecast +
activity decisions with si + present, future model answers). 4 performance targets + 3 linguistic skills, 27 flashcards,
16 quiz questions, 63 glossary entries, typed trainer (weather phrases, weather verbs, pass it on, temperatures in words incl.
bajo cero), "What is the speaker doing?" drill (16), 3 practice tasks (postcard, oral incl. role-play, reading). Rainfall bars
are rounded approximations (Santiago ≈ 1 800 mm … Almería ≈ 200 mm). localStorage prefix `es13-`.
