## I.1 contents

Labs: ¿Tú o usted? (12 people on a singular/plural × informal/formal grid) · Question wheel (15 answers, 12 question
words) · Mi ficha (live ID card and paragraph: numbers, veintiún, nationality agreement, y/e) · El árbol familiar
(11-person tree quiz) · Collect & fit (read Spanish interview answers, record study vs work hours, fitted line) ·
¿Qué dices? (12 social situations). 16 performance targets and linguistic skills, 27 flashcards, 16 quiz questions,
53 glossary entries, typed grammar trainer (5 types), "who's being addressed?" drill, 3 practice tasks (email, oral
conversation, reading comprehension).
