Scoil, pointí
agus post.
School, points and a job.

Your subjects, the school rules, college and the CAO, the job you'd like, and taking a phone message at work. Learn the phrases, master the grammar that catches people out (seacht n-ábhar, níos suimiúla, i m'innealtóir, dúirt sé go…), and practise answers you can actually say.

Ón siollabas · From the syllabus

Spriocanna · Goals

Quoted from the syllabus (Topic 2), with an English summary. Tick each one when you're confident. Saved on this device.

"Baineann cúrsaí scoile agus fostaíochta go dlúth le saol an duine óig." School and work matter a lot in a young person's life, so the syllabus wants you to talk about them, give your opinions and swap views.

    The syllabus groups this topic into: 2.1 Ábhair scoile agus scrúduithe (subjects and exams) · 2.2 Rialacha (rules) · 2.3 An Tríú Leibhéal (third level) · 2.4 Cúrsaí Oibre (work, including phone messages). At Higher Level you work on the same topics in more depth. Example phrases on this page marked "siollabas" come straight from the syllabus.

    Interactive

    Labs

    Survey the class, sort the rules, count your CAO points, find a career and pass on a message. Everything updates live.

    Suirbhé ábhar

    Survey your year group: "Cén t-ábhar is fearr leat?" Ask ten students at a time and watch the percentages settle. Then say how many subjects you do and compare two of them.

    Suirbhé
    1Slide the number of subjects from 6 to 7. Why does "ábhar" become "n-ábhar"?
    2Compare two of your own subjects. Which adjectives change in "níos …"?
    3After 200 students, report the winner in Irish: "Is fearr le …% de na daltaí …".
    Rialacha na scoile

    Sort the school rules: is it something you're not allowed to do (Níl cead agat…) or something you must do (Caithfidh tú…)? Then give your opinion of the rule.

    Mo thuairim (my opinion)
    Sorter
    1Sort all twelve. Which word comes after "Níl cead agat" and "Caithfidh tú"?
    2Notice the word order: "tobac a chaitheamh", "an éide scoile a chaitheamh". The object comes first.
    3Pick the rule you like least and argue against it out loud for 30 seconds.
    Pointí CAO

    Pick grades for seven subjects. Your best six count on the CAO points scale, with 25 bonus points for Higher Level Maths at H6 or better. Compare your total with an example course.

    Cúrsa (example course, illustrative points)
    1Change Maths from H6 to H7. Why do you lose far more than 9 points?
    2Drop one subject to a low grade. Why does your total sometimes not change?
    3Say your plan out loud with "Tá súil agam go bhfaighidh mé na pointí…".
    Slite beatha

    Say what kind of work you like. The chart ranks fourteen jobs by how well they match, and the Irish tells you what you'd like to be, with "i mo" + séimhiú.

    1Set "outdoors" to 10 and "technology" to 0. Which syllabus sentences appear?
    2Look at "i mo mhúinteoir" and "i m'altra". When does "mo" become "m'"?
    3Answer out loud: "Cén post ba mhaith leat amach anseo? Cén fáth?"
    An guthphost

    You work in an office. A caller leaves a voicemail for your manager, Máire. Pass the message on: change the pronoun, and use go / nach + the right tense.

    1Switch the caller from a man to a woman. Which small words change?
    2Compare "Glaofaidh mé" with "go nglaofadh sé". What two things changed?
    3Make up your own message and pass it on in Irish.
    An comhrá

    Pick an oral exam question about school or work and build your answer from the phrases. The meters show length, range of tenses and linking words: a rough guide, not official marks.

    Ceist (question)
    1Build an answer that uses at least three tenses.
    2Say it aloud and time it. Aim for about 30 seconds per question.
    3Swap in your own details: your subjects, your course, your dream job.

    Exam prep

    How Irish is assessed, how this topic comes up, and practice with feedback.

    An Bhéaltriail (oral) · 240 marks, 40%

    Common to Higher and Ordinary Level, around Easter of 6th year. This topic is the heart of the comhrá (conversation).

    Páipéar 1 · 160 marks, 27%

    Cluastuiscint (listening) 60 + Ceapadóireacht (writing) 100.

    Páipéar 2 · 200 marks, 33%

    HL: two reading comprehensions 50 + 50, prose 30, poetry 30, extra literature 40. (OL: prose 50, poetry 50.)

    Top tips for this topic

    1. Know your own subjects in Irish, and say how many: "Tá seacht n-ábhar ar siúl agam" (siollabas). 7–10 add an urú.
    2. Always give a reason: "Is fearr liom Bitheolaíocht mar…". Compare with níos … ná: "Tá Béarla níos suimiúla ná Stair."
    3. For rules: Níl cead agat… (not allowed) and Caithfidh tú… (must), then your view: "Tá ciall leis an riail sin" (siollabas).
    4. Plans after school need the future and conditional: "Rachaidh mé…", "Ba mhaith liom…", "Tá súil agam go bhfaighidh mé na pointí" (siollabas).
    5. Jobs: i mo + séimhiú (i mo mhúinteoir), i m' before a vowel or fh (i m'altra, i m'fheirmeoir).
    6. Passing on a message: Dúirt sé go / nach… + the conditional for things that will happen: "Dúirt sé nach mbeadh sé ag an gcruinniú" (siollabas).
    7. In the oral, keep talking: if you don't know a word, describe it another way.
    8. The tasks here are practice tasks, not official SEC papers.
