## 1.1 contents

Labs: Tree diagram (fundamental principle) · Collect & fit (shuffle 3–5 cards, running proportion in order vs 1/n!,
estimate n! from data) · Slots (ⁿPᵣ with "Anna first / not first" restrictions) · Pick a team (ⁿCᵣ vs ⁿPᵣ, symmetry) ·
Pascal's triangle (tap a number; parents, formula, symmetry, row sums) · Restrictions (MATHS, PLANET, ROUTINE: none,
starts with vowel, together, apart, vowels together, starts and ends with vowel, with a valid example arrangement).
4 learning outcomes (OL and HL), 25 flashcards, 16 quiz questions, 37 glossary terms, 7 trainer types (exact integer
answers), "which tool?" drill, 3 long questions with marking schemes.

## 1.2 contents

Labs: Two dice (36-cell sample space, 8 events incl. OR/AND, P(E) and P(E′)) · Collect & estimate (running relative
frequency for a die, a coin and a mystery spinner with a hidden P(red) from 0.15–0.45 that students estimate, then reveal) ·
Venn diagram (class of 30, sliders for each region, 7 shadable regions, addition rule, mutually exclusive check) ·
Bag and tree (r red, b blue, with/without replacement, 5 events, path products, P(2nd red | 1st red) v P(2nd red),
1 000-pair simulation) · Screening test (1 000-person grid, prevalence/sensitivity/false-positive sliders,
P(positive | condition) v P(condition | positive)) · Design a game (die game with prizes for 5 and 6, cost slider,
expected winnings/gain, fair price, running average over simulated games).
9 learning outcomes (1 FL background, 4 OL, 4 HL, verbatim), 28 flashcards, 18 quiz questions, 46 glossary terms,
9 trainer types (answers as fractions or decimals), "which rule?" drill (12 scenarios), 3 long questions with marking
schemes (two dice; Venn diagram with conditional parts; fair game with a tree).
