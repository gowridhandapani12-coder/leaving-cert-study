    Top exam tips for counting

    1. First ask: does order matter? Yes → arrangements (nPr, n!). No → selections (nCr).
    2. Draw the slots and write the number of choices in each before multiplying.
    3. Deal with restrictions first: fill the restricted places, then the rest.
    4. "Together": treat the group as one block, then multiply by the arrangements inside it.
    5. "Apart" or "at least one": use total − the opposite.
    6. "And" usually means multiply; separate cases ("or") mean add.
    7. Check with a small case you can list, and use the calculator's nPr and nCr keys.
    8. Show your working: marks are given for the method. Questions here are practice questions, not official SEC papers.
