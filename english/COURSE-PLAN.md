## Comprehension (Question A) contents

Three public-domain practice extracts, texts checked against Wikisource: J. M. Synge, The Aran Islands, Part I (1907, travel writing);
Jerome K. Jerome, "On Being Idle" (1886, comic essay); Emmeline Pankhurst, "Freedom or Death", Hartford (1913, persuasive speech).
Labs: Annotator (prose word-wrap, 3 lenses: evidence, style, tone; 35 notes) · Technique sorter (14 phrases, 4 boxes: imagery,
rhetorical devices, anecdote and analogy, opinion and claim) · Tone tracker (sections scored heavier-lighter, our reading) · Collect & fit
sentence lengths (real words or average word length per sentence, short sentences and exclamations coloured, fitted trend) · Genre
compass (radar of the syllabus's five language genres, overlay two texts) · Answer builder (25- and 15-mark questions, PCLM estimate).
6 syllabus outcomes quoted from 4.1.1, 4.2.1, 4.3.1, 4.4.1; 27 flashcards, 16 quiz questions, 58 glossary terms, text trainer (which
text / finish the sentence / word in context), "Which technique?" drill (16), 3 full Question A practice questions (10/15/25 marks)
with marking guides, Text bank tab (hash #formulae). localStorage prefix enc-.
