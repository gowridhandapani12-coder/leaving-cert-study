## Hamlet contents

Labs: Tension timeline (18 key scenes) · Inside Hamlet's head (7 soliloquies on a despair–resolve scale) ·
Relationship web (family, love and friendship, conflict, foils, deaths; lines spoken from the text) · Collect & fit
(real word counts per act for six themes, raw or per 100 lines, fitted trend) · Image sorter (12 quotations, 4 image
patterns) · Essay builder (3 questions, thesis and paragraph choice, PCLM estimate). 6 syllabus aims, 26 flashcards,
16 quiz questions, 51 glossary terms, 37-quote bank and quote trainer, "which theme?" drill, 3 long questions with
PCLM marking guides.

## Unseen poetry contents

Four public-domain practice poems, texts checked against Wikisource: Edward Thomas "Adlestrop" (1917), Christina Rossetti
"Remember" (1862), Sara Teasdale "There Will Come Soft Rains" (1920), Francis Ledwidge "Thomas McDonagh" (1917).
Labs: Annotator (3 lenses, 37 notes) · Image sorter (13 phrases, 4 boxes) · Mood map (sections scored darker-brighter, our
reading) · Collect & fit line shape (real words/letters per line, rhyme colours, fitted trend) · Theme compass (radar, 6 themes,
overlay two poems) · Answer builder (3 questions, 20-mark PCLM estimate). 6 syllabus aims (4.5.1, 5.4), 26 flashcards,
16 quiz questions, 59 glossary terms, line trainer (which poem / finish the line / which word rhymes), "Which device?" drill
(16), 3 long questions with marking guides, Poem bank tab (hash `#formulae`). localStorage prefix `enu-`.
