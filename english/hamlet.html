    Exam prep

    Where Hamlet sits in the exam, how answers are marked, and practice with feedback.

    Paper 1 · 200 marks

    Comprehending and composing: comprehension, functional writing and a composition.

    Paper 2, single text · 60 marks

    One question on your single text (Hamlet), with a choice of two. About an hour.

    Rest of Paper 2 · 140 marks

    Comparative study 70, unseen poem 20, prescribed poetry 50.

    Top exam tips for Hamlet

    1. Answer the question asked. Take a clear stance in your first paragraph and keep returning to the question's key words.
    2. Plan five or six paragraphs, each with one point, evidence, and analysis that links back to the question.
    3. Quote briefly and accurately. Short quotations woven into your sentences beat long block quotes.
    4. Analyse, don't retell. Plot summary earns little; say how and why Shakespeare creates an effect.
    5. Refer to drama: soliloquies, dramatic irony, foils, the play within the play, imagery.
    6. Range across the whole play, not just Act 1 and the famous scenes.
    7. Show a personal response: "I find…", "for me…" backed by evidence.
    8. Leave two minutes to proofread: spelling of names (Polonius, Guildenstern) counts under Mechanics. Questions here are practice questions, not official SEC papers.
