    Exam prep

    Where the unseen poem sits in the exam, how answers are marked, and practice with feedback.

    Paper 1 · 200 marks

    Comprehending and composing: comprehension, functional writing and a composition.

    Paper 2, unseen poem · 20 marks

    A poem you haven't studied, printed on the paper (Section C). About 20 minutes. Read the instructions on the day: the shape of the question can vary.

    Rest of Paper 2 · 180 marks

    Single text 60, comparative study 70, prescribed poetry 50.

    Top exam tips for the unseen poem

    1. Read the question first, then the poem twice: once for sense, once with a pen.
    2. Spend about 20 minutes: five reading and planning, fifteen writing.
    3. Answer in your first sentence. Use the question's key words.
    4. Write three or four focused paragraphs: point, short quotation, comment on effect.
    5. Use precise terms (sibilance, volta, pathetic fallacy), but always say what they do.
    6. Look for the turn: "But", "Yet", a new stanza, a question, a change of tense.
    7. Show a personal response: "I find…", "for me…", backed by the poem.
    8. Proofread the poet's name and every quotation. Questions here are practice questions, not official SEC papers.
