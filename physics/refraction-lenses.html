Light at a boundary

Three things light can do

Into denser: bends towards

Air → glass. Light slows down, r < i.

Into less dense: bends away

Glass → air. Light speeds up, r > i.

Past the critical angle: TIR

All light reflects back. i > C = 41.8° for glass.

Top exam tips for this chapter

  1. Write the formula first, then substitute, then the answer with its unit.
  2. Measure every angle from the normal, never from the surface.
  3. Check your calculator is in degrees before any sin or sin⁻¹.
  4. Real is positive: a virtual image gets a negative v; a diverging lens a negative f.
  5. For power, convert f to metres before P = 1/f.
  6. Ray diagrams: use a ruler, put arrows on rays, draw virtual rays dashed.
  7. Graphs: label both axes with units, choose a sensible scale, draw a line of best fit.
  8. "Define" means a full, precise sentence. Learn the glossary definitions word for word.
