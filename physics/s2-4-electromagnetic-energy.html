Light spreads over a sphere of area 4πd², so I = P / A becomes I = P / (4πd²). A graph of I against 1/d² is a straight line of slope P / 4π. The small intercept is light from the room.

Top exam tips for this chapter

  1. Write the formula first, then substitute, then the answer with its unit.
  2. Learn the order: radio, microwave, infrared, visible, UV, X-ray, gamma. Wavelength falls, frequency rises.
  3. Convert first: nm × 10⁻⁹ = m, cm ÷ 100 = m, MHz × 10⁶ = Hz, GHz × 10⁹ = Hz.
  4. All EM waves travel at c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s in a vacuum (and almost the same in air).
  5. Ionising means it can remove electrons from atoms. Name X-rays and gamma rays; for UV say "high-energy UV".
  6. To explain dispersion: n depends on colour, so each colour refracts differently: violet most, red least.
  7. Irradiance is in W/m²; power is in W. I = P / A, so P = I × A.
  8. For "impact on life", give a specific effect, its cause, and say where your information came from.
