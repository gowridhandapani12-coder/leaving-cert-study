Top exam tips for this chapter

  1. Write the formula first, then substitute, then the answer with its unit.
  2. Approaching: c − u, so f′ goes up. Moving away: c + u, so f′ goes down. Check your answer makes sense.
  3. u is the speed of the source; c is the speed of the wave (340 m/s for sound unless you're told otherwise).
  4. Convert km/h to m/s first: divide by 3.6.
  5. Wavefront sketches: each circle is centred where the source was when it made that wave. Bunched on the side it's moving towards.
  6. "Define the Doppler effect" needs two ideas: apparent change in frequency and relative motion.
  7. For light, use c = 3.0 × 10⁸ m/s and Δλ / λ ≈ u / c. Longer λ means moving away.
  8. For an application, name it, say what is measured (the frequency shift) and what it tells you (a speed).
